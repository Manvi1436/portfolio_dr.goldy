About Dr. Goldy Jain

BPT MPT (Ortho) MIAP

Dr. Goldy Jain is a dedicated physiotherapist with over 5 years of clinical experience in treating musculoskeletal conditions, sports injuries, and post-operative rehabilitation.

She believes in a patient-first approach, combining modern techniques with personalized care plans to help patients recover faster and live pain-free.
